Category: general
Question: Solve the following math problem. Answer with just the letter.

Problem: a man covers a certain distance q in a train . if the train moved 4 km / hr faster , it would take 30 min less . if it moved 2 km / hr slower , it would take 20 mins more . find the distance ?

A. 200 km
B. 50 km
C. 20 km
D. 60 km
E. 80 km

Answer:
Expected: D
Predicted: D
Raw response: The problem asks us to find the distance $q$ covered by a train. Let $v$ be the initial speed of the train in km/hr and $t$ be the initial time taken in hours.

The relationship between distance, speed, and time is:
$q = v \cdot t$

We are given two scenarios:
1. If the train moves 4 km/hr faster, it takes 30 minutes (0.5 hours) less:
$q = (v + 4)(t - 0.5)$

2. If the train moves 2 km/hr slower, it takes 20 minutes (1/3 hours) more:
$q = (v - 2)(t + 1/3)$

Now, we set up the equations using $q = vt$:
From scenario 1:
$vt = (v + 4)(t - 0.5)$
$vt = vt - 0.5v + 4t - 2$
$0 = -0.5v + 4t - 2$
Multiply by 2 to simplify:
$v - 8t = -4$  (Equation 1)

From scenario 2:
$vt = (v - 2)(t + 1/3)$
$vt = vt + \frac{1}{3}v - 2t - \frac{2}{3}$
$0 = \frac{1}{3}v - 2t - \frac{2}{3}$
Multiply by 3 to simplify:
$v - 6t = 2$  (Equation 2)

Now, solve the system of linear equations (Equation 1 and Equation 2):
Subtract Equation 1 from Equation 2:
$(v - 6t) - (v - 8t) = 2 - (-4)$
$2t = 6$
$t = 3$ hours

Substitute $t = 3$ back into Equation 2:
$v - 6(3) = 2$
$v - 18 = 2$
$v = 20$ km/hr

Finally, calculate the distance $q$:
$q = v \cdot t = 20 \cdot 3 = 60$ km

Comparing this result to the given options:
A. 200 km
B. 50 km
C. 20 km
D. 60 km
E. 80 km

The correct option is D.

Answer: D

Problem: the speeds of three asteroids were compared . asteroids x - 13 and y - 14 were observed for identical durations , while asteroid z - 15 was observed for 2 seconds longer . during its period of observation , asteroid y - 14 traveled three times the distance x - 13 traveled , and therefore y - 14 was found to be faster than x - 13 by 1000 kilometers per second . asteroid z - 15 had an identical speed as that of x - 13 , but because z - 15 was observed for a longer period , it traveled five times the distance x - 13 traveled during x - 13 ' s inspection . asteroid x - 13 traveled how many kilometers during its observation ?

A. 250
B. 1,600 / 3
C. 1,000
D. 1,500
E. 2,500

Answer:
Expected: A
Predicted: A
Raw response: To solve the problem, we define the following variables for each asteroid:
- Asteroid X: speed $v_x$, observation time $t_x$, distance $d_x = v_x \cdot t_x$
- Asteroid Y: speed $v_y$, observation time $t_y$, distance $d_y = v_y \cdot t_y$
- Asteroid Z: speed $v_z$, observation time $t_z$, distance $d_z = v_z \cdot t_z$

From the problem description, we can establish the following relationships:
1.  **Identical durations for X and Y:** $t_x = t_y = t$
2.  **Z was observed for 2 seconds longer:** $t_z = t + 2$
3.  **Y traveled three times the distance of X:** $d_y = 3 \cdot d_x$
4.  **Y was faster than X by 1000 km/s:** $v_y = v_x + 1000$
5.  **Z had the same speed as X:** $v_z = v_x$
6.  **Z traveled five times the distance of X:** $d_z = 5 \cdot d_x$

Now, we use these relationships to set up equations:
- From (3) and (4):
  $v_y \cdot t_y = 3 \cdot (v_x \cdot t_x)$
  $(v_x + 1000) \cdot t = 3 \cdot v_x \cdot t$
  Since $t \neq 0$, we can divide both sides by $t$:
  $v_x + 1000 = 3v_x$
  $1000 = 2v_x$
  $v_x = 500 \text{ km/s}$

- From (6) and (5):
  $v_z \cdot t_z = 5 \cdot (v_x \cdot t_x)$
  $v_x \cdot (t + 2) = 5 \cdot v_x \cdot t$
  Since $v_x \neq 0$, we can divide both sides by $v_x$:
  $t + 2 = 5t$
  $2 = 4t$
  $t = 0.5 \text{ seconds}$

Finally, we calculate the distance traveled by asteroid X during its observation:
$d_x = v_x \cdot t = 500 \cdot 0.5 = 250 \text{ km}$

Comparing this result to the given options:
A. 250

E. 2,500
